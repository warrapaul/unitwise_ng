Tenants Module: UI/UX Review
A. Global issues (apply across all screens)

Layout & navigation

Use one navigation pattern the bottom nav “More” icon (≡) .
Show the bottom nav consistently. It is missing on the wizard steps 1-4 and the Tenants list but present on the other screens.
Truncated page titles (“Add existing user as t…”) must be shortened or allowed to wrap.
Replace generic titles (“Tenant detail”, “Lease detail”) with the actual entity name.
The property switcher is cramped. “Highridge / Homes” wraps onto two right-aligned lines
The notification bell has a dot but no count, and the dot is too small to read.
Header icon buttons (hamburger, bell) are about 32px. The minimum touch target is 44×44px.
Scope is stated three times on list screens: the header switcher, the “Filters in Acacia Court” chip, and the “Acacia Court only · Show all” strip. Keep one.
“Back” and “Back to tenants” are used inconsistently. Pick one pattern.

Visual design

Green-on-green and white-on-pale-green contrast is too low. Fix all gray helper text, labels, and inactive nav items to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI components).
Helper and label text is about 11-12px. Raise the minimum to 14px.
Input borders are too faint against the translucent gradient cards. Strengthen them to 3:1.
Remove the translucent gradient cards on a gradient background. Use solid surfaces with clear elevation.
Stop nesting cards (page → container card → item card). It wastes about 24px of width per side on mobile.
There is a visible background seam at about 790px on the wizard screens. Fix the gradient/background height.
Type hierarchy is weak. Section titles, sub-labels, and values are too close in size and weight.
Status colors are doing all the work. Add icons or text so meaning doesn’t depend on color alone.

Buttons

Too many outlined pill buttons compete on the same screen. Define a clear hierarchy: primary, secondary, tertiary/text, destructive.
Use one button order everywhere. Cancel is far left on Image 1 but sits beside the primary on Images 7-8. (CANCEL ALWAYS ON THE LEFT SIDE, PREFFERABLY FAR RIGHT ON THE MOBILE DEVICES)
Don’t show three buttons (Cancel, Back, Next) in a row. Cancel -move it to the header (ADVICE FUTHER ON THIS)
Disabled buttons (Send invitation) fail contrast and give no reason. Add helper text or keep them enabled and validate on press.

Forms

Use one convention for required vs. optional. Currently asterisks, “(optional)”, and nothing are mixed, sometimes on the same form.
Do not use placeholders as values or hints (“Agreed KES 25,000”, “No rent set”, “The code they gave”). Placeholders look like entered data.
Native <select> and custom selects are mixed. The arrow styles differ. Use one component.
Dates show as mm/dd/yyyy (US format). Use a Kenyan format (dd/mm/yyyy or “7 Nov 2026”) and a consistent date picker.
The currency pattern is inconsistent: “(KES)” in labels, a “KES” prefix in Record payment, and no currency at all in the lease form. Use one prefix pattern.
Money inputs have no thousands separators, and number inputs are used for amounts. Format as the user types, with a numeric keyboard.
Set inputmode, type, and autocomplete on all fields (tel, email, numeric).
Phone formats are inconsistent: +254712345678, 254700000205, 07883749837, +254700000701. Normalize to one display format, e.g. +254 700 000 205.
Make phone numbers tappable (tel:), and emails too (mailto:).
Validate the phone number. 07883749837 has 11 digits and was accepted.
Show validation after blur or submit, not on first load (the “Room is required.” error on Step 2).
Cancel on a partly filled form needs a discard confirmation.
Textareas show a resize handle. Disable it on mobile.

Content & terminology

Brand casing is inconsistent: “UnitWise”, “Unitwise”, “UnitWise ID”.
Use one term for the occupant: Tenant, Renter, and User are mixed. (USE TENANT)
Use one term for the housing unit: Room vs. unit.(USE ROOM)
Use one name for the lease screens: “Leases”, “Lease agreements”, and “All leases” are all in use.
Use one verb set for money actions: “Pay”, “Record payment”, and “Record deposit” are all used.(USE RECORD PAYMENT FOR ADMINS, AND PAY FOR TENANTS)
Replace internal jargon (“Frozen”, “Intended”, “Verify and reserve”, “Reissues the agreement”) with plain labels.
Casual copy (“worth asking while they are here”, “Who they are”) is out of place in a production tool. Use neutral headings.
Capitalization is inconsistent (“Next Of Kin”, “paul wara waaaa”). Use sentence case and auto-capitalize names.

Missing states and patterns

No loading or offline states shown.
No success confirmation after creating a tenant.
No empty states with CTAs (“No lease agreements yet”, “None recorded”).
No confirmation dialogs for destructive or irreversible actions. (ALREADY THERE)
No search or sort on any list. (MOST ALREADY IMPLEMENTED)
