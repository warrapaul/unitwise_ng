Final UI/UX Review: Property Management System (Mobile)

NOTE AM USING DUMMY DATA SO IF ANY ISSUE IS RELATED TO THE STORED DB DATA, DONT FORCE CODE MODIFUCATIONS
BUT CONFIRM FIRST THAT THE ISSUES IS ONLY BECAUSE OF THE STORED DATA



1. Critical (fix before production)

Data and status contradictions

Meter readings count conflicts across screens: Dashboard says 5, Rent & billing says 4, Building performance says “4 readings due”, Meter readings page says 5.
Meter readings list shows duplicate rows (1-3 Esther twice, 2-1 Grace twice) with no way to tell them apart. Show the meter or utility name.
Rooms 1-3 and 2-1 show “Overdue 1 day” while Sep arrears are 62 days late. Show the oldest overdue age, not only the current month’s.
All payments marks Grace, Kevin and Lucy’s Sep/Oct bills as “Pending”, but the Overdue portfolio shows them overdue. Use one status engine everywhere.
“Who owes most” lists Lucy (2-3) and Kevin (2-2), but those rooms are vacant. Label them “Former tenant” or fix the data.
Arrears by age shows 12 tenants (3+5+4), but only 5 tenants appear in “Who owes most”. Reconcile this.
Overdue KPI shows 3 tenants, but arrears show 5.
“Paid this month: 0” while payments exist in the list.
Dashboard says 1 current lease but 3 active tenants. Explain or fix it.
“Outstanding” (this month) and “Arrears” (all-time) are never distinguished. Label the time scope on every figure.

Risky actions without safeguards

“Confirm month” is irreversible but has no explanation or confirmation dialog.
“Mark paid” on the Overdue portfolio has no confirmation, no amount and no date.
“Apply one reading to everyone” bulk-charges all tenants with no preview.
“Sign out” sits right under the profile link with no confirmation.
Bulk “Select” checkboxes have no bulk action bar.
2. Global and cross-screen issues

Navigation and structure

Add a bottom tab bar (Home, Rent, Payments, Tenants, More) for frequent tasks. A hamburger alone is too slow.
Add a page title and back button on every inner screen. Rent per room, All payments, Payments to confirm, All rooms, Meter readings and Overdue portfolio have none.
The header doesn’t show the current page name.
Merge overlapping pages: Rent per room, All rooms, All payments, Arrears, Overdue portfolio and “Rooms with a balance” show the same data.
Remove “All rooms” and “Record payment” from the Payments submenu. One is a list, the other an action.
“Payments to confirm” should surface a badge count in the menu.
Limit menu depth to two levels.

Menu inconsistencies

The menu is named “Arrears & reports” but the page is “Overdue portfolio”. Pick one name.
Dashboard and “Tenants & leases” both look active at once.
“Tenants & leases” has a right chevron while other groups have down chevrons.
The drawer avatar shows “U” but the profile shows “AO”. The header avatar also shows “U”.
The notification dot on the hamburger is ambiguous. Move it to a bell icon with a notification page.
The drawer has no app logo or name.

Building and month filters

The “Showing figures for Acacia Court only” banner repeats on every screen. Replace it with a compact chip inside the switcher.
Add prev/next arrows around the month picker, not only the native picker.
Persist the selected month across screens.
Block or flag future months.
Remove the “Load” button on Meter readings. Reload when the month changes.
Don’t repeat “Acacia Court” on every room card when one building is filtered.

Consistency

“Record payment” sits in a different place on each screen. Use one position, ideally a floating action button or sticky bottom bar.
Two filled green primary buttons compete in one view (Record payment and Open rent per room).
Use one primary action per screen.
Button heights, radii and font sizes vary. Define one button scale.
Native inputs on Meter readings have square corners and don’t match the rest of the app.
Cards sit inside cards inside cards. Flatten them.
Group headers (“floor1”) are full-bleed grey bars that don’t match the card style.
Unify terminology:
Owing, Outstanding, Balance and Due → pick one.
“Due” is ambiguous, since it can mean amount or date. Use “Billed”.
Room, Unit and Location → pick one.
Record, Enter and Save → pick one.
Mark paid and Record payment → pick one.
Pending, Overdue, Not yet due, Not billed, Completed, Paid → define a single status set.
Properties and Buildings → clarify.

Formatting

Date formats are mixed: mm/dd/yyyy, “Nov 6”, “6 Nov 2026” and “Sep 2026”. Use dd/mm/yyyy or “6 Nov 2026” everywhere, including pickers.
Money formats are mixed: “KES 23,740”, “23740” and “9500”. Use thousand separators everywhere, including inputs.
“KES” repeats on every figure. Show it once per card or use a smaller prefix.
Right-align and use tabular figures for amounts.
Fix grammar: “Overdue 1 days”, “floor1” and “m3” (should be m³).
Amounts and currency break across lines (“KES / 4,000”). Use non-breaking spaces.
Remove trailing commas such as “Nov 6,”.
Room numbers like “2-1” read like a ratio or date. Use “Room 2-1” or “F2-R1”.
Placeholder casing is inconsistent (“E.G. QGR4T6Y7U8” vs “e.g. QGR4T6Y7U8”).

Information density

Don’t show zero-value cards (Paid 0, Not yet due 0, Not billed 0). Hide them or collapse them into one line.
Remove orphan half-width cards (“To confirm”, “Payments to confirm”, “Vacant”).
Make stat tiles tappable filters and give them a visible selected state, or make them non-interactive.
Stop repeating the same numbers (Collected, Collection progress, Building performance and Rent & billing all show 10,500 / 78,740 / 13.3%).
The dashboard is about 2,500px tall. Cut it to KPIs, Needs attention, a trend and a short list.
3. Screen-by-screen

Login

Make “Sign in” a full-width button.
Add a logo and app name.
Inactive tab (“Phone code”) has weak contrast and doesn’t look tappable.
The phone field needs a format hint and should accept 07XX and +254 and normalize them.
Add inline error states and a clear message for wrong credentials.
Add show/hide labels and the correct autocomplete attributes.
Vertical edge lines show at both sides of the screen. Remove this layout artifact.
Raise the minimum password length. The sample password is 6 characters.

Change password

Show the password rules up front.
Add a strength meter and live match validation.
Explain why the change is forced.
Add a “Sign out” escape.
The description text wraps narrower than the card. Use the full width.
Make the button full width.

Dashboard

Move “Needs your attention” above the KPI grid.
Give each attention item a distinct icon and severity. All three use the same icon.
Don’t truncate the overdue list (“and 1 more”) without a count and tap target.
KPI cards need tap-through to their detail.
Delete “Collection progress”, since it duplicates the Collected KPI.
Delete “Building performance” when only one building is filtered.
Chart: hide empty months (Jun–Aug), add value labels or tap tooltips, enlarge the axis text (about 10px now), and add a text alternative.
“All rooms / All buildings” look like buttons but act as toggles. Make them a segmented control.
“Rent per room” shows only three cards with no “View all” link.
Overdue pill is the same colour for 1 day and 62 days. Add severity tiers.
Tenants & leases: merge “Current leases”, “Signed and active” and “Active tenants”, and drop the zeros.
The empty “Upcoming lease endings” card is oversized. Make it a single line.

Sidebar

Section labels are tiny and low-contrast.
Icons are thin and faint.
Sub-item tap targets are under 44px.
Collapse groups by default except the active one.
Put Sign out in the profile menu, not next to it.

Rent & billing

The “Monthly billing cycle” stepper is broken. Steps 1, 3 and 5 are unlabeled, numbers float outside the boxes, and the layout looks like a wrap error. Use a vertical or horizontal stepper with all five labelled steps and one clearly marked current step.
The subtitle “Generate → readings…” is jargon. Rewrite it.
“Overdue” KPI duplicates “Outstanding”. Remove one.
Arrears bars are misaligned: the 90+ row has a shorter track. Align all bars and amounts in fixed columns.
Make each arrears-by-age row tappable, to a filtered list.
“Who owes most” needs a rank, Call, WhatsApp and Remind actions, and a “former tenant” flag where relevant.
“Rooms with a balance” repeats “Who owes most”. Remove it or merge.
The “Status” label above a pill is unnecessary.

Rent per room

Warning copy (“they move to next month’s bill”) is unclear. State exactly what happens.
“Confirm month” is outlined and “Enter readings” is filled, which hides the destructive action’s weight. Show the consequence.
The status legend (Paid / Part paid / Overdue) should be filter chips, not tiny text with dots.
The meter readings section here duplicates the Meter readings page. Link to it instead.
Each meter card has excessive empty space. Make it a compact row.
Collapse “Readings entered” with a visible count. It’s easy to miss.
“Occupied only” checkbox and “9 vacant rooms hidden · Show” do the same thing. Keep one.
Room cards: phone is bolder than the tenant name, so fix the hierarchy.
The tiny chevron beside Remind has no clear target.
Put “Record payment” and “Remind” in a consistent order.
Add a per-room overflow menu.

Record payment (bottom sheet)

Remove the amount’s full balance prefill, or label it “Full balance” with a chip to change it.
Add thousand separators.
Date is in US format and conflicts with the summary (“6 Nov”).
The summary should show the balance after this payment, not only the current one.
Make the transaction code required when M-Pesa is selected, and validate its format inline.
Add a drag handle and a sticky footer for the buttons.
X and Cancel are redundant. Keep one.
Use a lighter backdrop. The heavy blur is distracting.

Meter reading sheet

Add the unit (m³), rate, a consumption preview and a calculated charge preview.
Validate that current ≥ previous.
Lock “Previous” unless it’s the first reading, and explain why when editable.
Show last reading date and photo or note support if needed.
Use a numeric keyboard.

All payments

The card title says “Rent payments”, but unpaid bills are listed. Rename to “Rent bills” or filter to real payments.
“Search ▾” is really a filter. Rename it and add a real search field.
“Overdue only” looks like a primary button. Make it a toggle chip with a visible on/off state.
Group or sort by month or status.
Rows need a chevron or tap affordance.
Summary text wraps badly. Use a two-line layout: amount owing on one line, last payment on the next.
Replace pagination with infinite scroll or “Load more”. “Showing 13 of 13” doesn’t need paging and rows controls.
Add the year to dates.

Payments to confirm

The empty state should come first. The “Record a payment you received” form currently pushes it to the bottom.
Use “—” and “0” consistently (“Resolved” shows a dash).
“Find match” is unclear. Say what happens next.
This form duplicates “Record a rent payment”. Explain the difference or merge them.
Show parsed results after the message is pasted, so the autofill is visible.

All rooms

Seven tiles take about a third of the screen. Replace them with one summary line and filter chips.
Vacant cards are as tall as occupied ones. Compress them.
Show asking rent and an “Add tenant” action on vacant rooms.
Add filters (status, floor) and sort.
The search placeholder lists “building”, which is redundant when filtered to one.

Record a rent payment (full form)

Pick the tenant first, then show their room, balance and months owing.
Paired fields are unrelated (Late fee next to Transaction code). Reorder them logically.
Helper text under Transaction code misaligns the columns.
“Late fee” has no KES prefix and no explanation. Is it included in the amount?
Add overpayment and partial-payment messaging.
Add a confirmation summary like the sheet version has.
The “Payment message” paste box before the main fields pushes them down. Collapse it into an “Autofill from M-Pesa message” link.

Meter readings page

Two save patterns (per-row “Save” and bulk “Save 0 readings”) confuse users. Use one.
The per-row Save looks disabled. Strengthen it.
The disabled bulk button is very low contrast.
Input fields are too narrow and don’t fill the card.
Placeholders such as “Opening reading” stand in for labels.
“Billing timing: Prior month arrear” is jargon and gets truncated. Rewrite it and widen the dropdown.
“Consumption” and “Amount” in “Apply one reading” don’t explain which wins. Add helper text.
The collapse chevron button is unlabeled.
Add a progress count (“1 of 6 done”) and keyboard “Next” navigation between fields.

Overdue portfolio

Group by tenant, with total owed, oldest age and a expandable month breakdown. Esther and Grace each appear three times now.
Add summary totals and a sort by amount or days late at the top.
“Outstanding” shows “9500” and “25500” with no currency or separators.
“Search 1 in Acacia Court” and “Clear search” are a filter, not a search. Rename.
The checkbox is first in each card and takes prime space. Move it to a corner and show a selection bar.
“Covers Sep 2026” is underlined like a link. Make it plain text or an actual link.
“Reminded: –” should say “Never” or show the date and channel.
“Remind” needs channel choice (SMS or WhatsApp) and a message preview.
Add Call and WhatsApp for the phone number. The copy icon alone isn’t enough.
Add export and print (this is a report).
4. Accessibility
Secondary grey text and pastel pills (brown on pink, green on pale green) look below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 ratio. Test and darken.
Status relies on colour alone. Add icons or text. “Part paid” and “Overdue” are nearly the same brown/red.
Many tap targets (Record, Remind, chevrons, checkboxes, Save) are below 44×44px.
Small text: chart axes (~10px), labels (~11–12px). Set a 14px minimum for body and 12px for captions.
Add visible focus states and keyboard order for forms.
Don’t use placeholders as labels.
Connect error messages to fields with aria attributes and announce toasts with aria-live.
Use a proper heading hierarchy. Several screens start at card titles with no H1.
Provide a table alternative for the chart.
Respect system font scaling and test at 200%.
5. Missing states and feedback
loading, error states and offline handling for every list and form.
Success toasts with Undo after Record payment, Save reading, Confirm month and Remind.
Inline validation, not only on submit.
Useful empty states with a next action (for example, “No payments to confirm. Tenants’ reports appear here.”).
Disabled buttons should explain why they’re disabled.
Unsaved-changes warning when closing sheets or leaving forms.
6. Suggested priority order
Data and status contradictions.
Confirmation on destructive and bulk actions.
Fix the billing stepper and unify terminology and status labels.
Page titles, back buttons, bottom nav and the consolidated filter chip.
Tap targets, contrast and date and money formats.
Merge duplicate screens and trim the dashboard.
Form improvements, validation and success and empty states.
